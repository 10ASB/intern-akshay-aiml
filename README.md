# AI & ML Internship — Akshay

This repository follows the shared **4-Week Core AI & ML Internship Program**: 20 working days, organized by the roadmap's actual weekly themes and daily deliverables.

## Roadmap

### Week 1 — Foundations & Classical ML (Part 1)
- **Day 01 — Onboarding, ML Landscape & the Data Stack:** AI vs ML vs DL vs Generative AI; learning paradigms; ML lifecycle; production concerns; Git/GitHub; NumPy and Pandas drills; Iris and Titanic insights.
- **Day 02 — The Maths That Actually Matters:** linear algebra, probability and statistics, calculus, gradient descent and learning-rate experiments.
- **Day 03 — Data Preprocessing, EDA & Feature Engineering:** missing values, outliers, encoding, scaling, leakage, data splitting, pipelines and EDA.
- **Day 04 — Linear & Logistic Regression (from scratch):** regression, cost functions, assumptions, regularisation and scratch implementations.
- **Day 05 — Model Evaluation & Mini-Project 1 Kickoff:** evaluation metrics, cross-validation, class imbalance and project assignment.
- Friday demo and review.

### Week 2 — Classical ML (Part 2) & First End-to-End Project
- **Day 06 — Distance & Margin Based Models:** KNN, Naive Bayes and SVM.
- **Day 07 — Trees & Bagging:** decision trees and random forests.
- **Day 08 — Boosting & Hyperparameter Tuning:** AdaBoost, gradient boosting, XGBoost/LightGBM and tuning.
- **Day 09 — Unsupervised Learning & Dimensionality Reduction:** clustering, PCA, t-SNE/UMAP and anomaly detection.
- **Day 10 — Mini-Project 1:** build, polish and present the project.

### Week 3 — Deep Learning
- **Day 11 — Neural Networks from Scratch:** forward propagation, backpropagation, activations and optimisation in NumPy.
- **Day 12 — PyTorch & Training Deep Models Properly:** tensors, autograd, training loops, regularisation and experiment logging.
- **Day 13 — Computer Vision:** CNNs, transfer learning and ResNet18.
- **Day 14 — NLP: From TF-IDF to Transformers:** text processing, baselines and transformer fine-tuning.
- **Day 15 — LLMs, Embeddings & RAG:** prompting, embeddings, semantic search and retrieval-augmented generation.
- Friday demo; capstone team formation and problem selection.

### Week 4 — Deployment, MLOps, Responsible AI & Capstone
- **Day 16 — Serving Models:** FastAPI, app UI, model serialization and Docker basics.
- **Day 17 — MLOps Essentials & Responsible AI:** MLflow, versioning, drift, fairness and explainability.
- **Day 18 — Capstone Build (Day 1):** sprint planning, data pipeline and baseline model.
- **Day 19 — Capstone Build (Day 2):** model improvement, evaluation, API/UI, documentation and presentation preparation.
- **Day 20 — Final Demo Day & Wrap-Up:** final presentation, technical viva, code review and feedback.
